# Before narrowing: markets, technology and price events

Use the applicable sections at the first comparison and when new evidence reopens a route. The decision is where to spend the next research call, not which issuer to endorse.

## 1. Search different economics, then enumerate the businesses

O00 records three separate fields: **investable securities** (venue, class, geography), **industries allowed for selection**, and **evidence universe** (including private firms, other exchanges, buyers and substitutes). A hard sector limit bounds ranking. When themes are examples and the user asks about other industries, keep any venue constraint and compare unrelated eligible industries too. A genuinely single-issuer or release assignment stays bounded; consult relevant outside evidence without starting a screen. Name examples as leads, not mandatory picks.

A03 starts with changes in the *payer's need* and market growth or contraction. Search in three passes: split the named markets into buyer/application/product economics; follow suppliers, customers, rivals, substitutes and scarce inputs in both directions; then, for broad mandates, look outside that spending cycle. Use independent demand drivers such as food consumption, medical procedures, freight utilization or industrial replacement, selected for their evidence, not because a commodity recently rose. If sectors are expressly restricted, an outside market can still supply input costs, substitution or a counterexample. Explain the boundary instead of silently changing it.

Build two discovery lists independently: **(a)** a dated eligible-security directory or broad classification/screener joined to product terms and filing descriptions, including small/underfollowed suppliers; **(b)** physical-chain evidence from procurement, qualification documents, competitor filings, equipment specifications or industry statistics. Reconcile their union, aliases, cross-listings, comparators and omissions. Do not infer eligibility from a symbol or infer complete coverage from search results. If only a partial directory or current-vintage list is available, record its date and unmatched scope. For a named missed business, supply a sourced opportunity card or a reasoned identity/scope disposition and an evidence role. Do not silently put it back on an unread watchlist.

A05/A06 sketch a **dated common-unit balance** for each material challenger: usable opening stock (net of inaccessible inventory and required floor), qualified inflow including substitution/imports, and financeable consumption/exports. Avoid counting an import twice. Calculate when the buffer binds and when the next qualified response arrives; if not computable, give the missing observation and break-even inflow, usage or capacity. In services use usable slots/hours, available capacity and queues, not imaginary storable units. Test both tightness and easing. A deficit is not a price forecast: contract resets, inventories, hedges, substitution and bargaining power determine which buyer or seller captures it and when it reaches earnings or cash.

**O00 acceptance:** before a second deep dive on a favored issuer, compare the best untested independent pool, an overlooked issuer or missing business model, and the next question on the leader. Show the paid-demand driver, balance or threshold, who captures value, counter-case, and information due by the price horizon. Record which question wins the next call and why. Count unique issuers, screened vs unexamined names and *independent spending/constraint drivers* separately from micro-market rows. Stop expansion when a distinct search source/driver has been tried and further retrieval adds no decision-changing mechanism, or document the exact boundary, resource exception and next discriminator. One shared capex budget split into ten products is not ten independent bets. No arbitrary completeness percentage or mandatory turnaround.

## 2. Commission the technology expert while admission is still open

**Trigger:** if a product generation, substitute, manufacturing process or launch delay could reverse a route's admission, share, margin or horizon, O00 sends a **clean A08 task before advance or deferral**, not only after selecting finalists. Supply the competing hypotheses, target customer and cutoff, not the desired winner or price. A08 reads the original relevant paper, chip/system specification, test protocol or public submission and the best rival's material; inspect methods, appendices and dates where material. Record sources actually read, inaccessible originals and conditions that could reverse the comparison. Unknown implementation details merit a bounded search; they do not erase positive evidence of progress.

Identify the economic unit and best available or forthcoming alternative for the customer doing the job. Trace the milestones that actually apply between a performance claim, independent measurement, qualification or permission, a funded purchase, and paid use. These steps differ by business; skip inapplicable steps and date the binding one. A leaked result can change the next task or what investors have heard. Record earliest accessible public artifact, author/origin, revision or deletion, test conditions, sample versus deployable product, performance per unit of customer cost, and the conditions where the result could reverse. Reposts are one origin. A published score can be observed as a submission without verifying its hardware, repeatability or a rival advantage. Seek a genuinely independent test/OEM or customer clue. Positive, adverse and no-change conclusions use the same evidence standard.

Translate only the supported difference into **customer economics**: cost per completed job, sustained speed, energy, compatibility, migration cost and best alternative; use a threshold if unpriced. Pass the implication to A09/A18/A10 for paid design slots, supply, attach/share and rival response, then to A12 for incremental units × realized contribution less launch/ramp cost. When the same advance may help two businesses, trace each payer, qualification, dated cash flow and incremental contribution separately. Internal cost savings can be real without an external customer. Eliminate internal transfers and count a shared input only once. Do not move all adoption dates together because one milestone improved.

Return **two decisions**: the chosen next-quarter/half/year operating implication, including explicit no-change where warranted, and what new information can alter beliefs inside the price horizon. An announced launch can support a real future ramp even if its revenue is outside the window; already-public news cannot supply a second surprise. If the decisive comparison is unresolved, record the specific competing test or customer action, owner and revisit date/checkpoint. No forecast of superiority merely because a leak circulated.

## 3. Explain a material move without manufacturing a cause

**Trigger:** a move material to ranking, a peer dislocation, or an explicit volatility question. A14 fixes the security, exact session/window, corporate actions, pre/postmarket convention, comparable industry peers and broad benchmark. When available check prior variability, volume and time of first move. Quantify issuer and comparison moves; a peer-relative residual is descriptive, not an identified shock. If price already moved before first verified publication, it cannot establish reaction to that publication. Daily closes rarely identify an intraday trigger.

A01 and A14 search issuer/customer/competitor and regulator disclosures, reliable contemporaneous news/wires, and public social sources relevant to this market (e.g. practitioner discussions, original posts, public benchmark submissions). Search the first-move window and preceding interval by event/product/issuer aliases; look for disconfirmations, edits and corrections. Record actual searches and dated results, including no accessible original, paywall, missing timezone or ambiguous first availability. Do not bypass access controls or treat an inaccessible forum as evidence of silence.

Use one ledger row per original claim: **event time | first public time and zone | retrieval/version | first observable move | original and echoes | what was already known | competing explanation | affected model node | permitted use**. A verified timestamp may establish that a narrative circulated; self-selected posts, engagement and several mirrors cannot identify representative positioning, conviction, direction of flows, physical demand or causation. Label observed disclosure, company claim, allegation and inference separately using the existing evidence classes; quarantine post-cutoff or unverifiable chronology for as-of claims. A material correction supersedes the claim only from its own public timestamp forward. A social post from an issuer is still an issuer-origin claim.

O00 resolves one of three actions: **change the operating path** only when operating evidence changes a dated driver; **change the recognition path** when documented new circulation/disclosure can affect expectations with a testable timing assumption; or **leave the point path unchanged** and log the unresolved narrative, alternate cause and cheapest discriminator. If the price changes but no event is established, revisit valuation at the allowed reference, not the demand forecast. Preserve frozen forecasts. This is a bounded event investigation, not continual monitoring, a sentiment score or a new gate.
