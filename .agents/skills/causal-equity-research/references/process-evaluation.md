# Evaluating and refining the research process

Read for method changes, audits and A20/A21/A22/A23 evaluation tasks. Ordinary stock research does not trigger a full benchmark rerun. The purpose is to improve decisions across mechanisms, not to make the method select a remembered winner.

For a requested audit of investment reasoning, start with the actual forecasts, underlying sources and decisions on the named economic aspects. Use the complete relevant cohort or a declared outcome-independent sample, including rejected and unresolved routes. Examine unsupported normalization, omitted forward balances, and missed changes beneath weak/flat metrics; retain cases where caution or continued deterioration is supported. Software tests, metadata checks and invented examples do not answer that audit. Distinguish an instruction/coverage review from an executed forecasting test; do not expand a focused audit into a general engineering benchmark.

## Select the population before observing success

Define the workflow failure under investigation and the evidence needed to distinguish it from a data-access failure, legitimate abstention, accounting error or price-recognition miss. User examples are discovery clues, not the test population. Use a declared universe, dates and inclusion/exclusion rule; keep eligible failures, flat outcomes and unavailable records in the denominator. Do not backfill missing cases with winners. Report every exclusion and retrieval failure.

For synthetic process work, select mechanism families before writing outcomes: customer/system economics, adoption intersections, qualified capacity, inventory and price response, costs/cash/financing, accounting quality, source chronology, evidence reuse, scheduling and recognition. Include favorable, adverse, no-change and insufficient-information decisions. Case count is engineering scope, not statistical sample sufficiency. Include counterfactual changes that should affect the decision and irrelevant changes that should not; actual execution of these transformations must be recorded rather than claimed from a plan.

For real historical work, choose issuer-periods by a reproducible rule independent of future returns. Preserve delistings, failures, corporate actions and chain/group dependencies. Apply vintage filters, train-only choices and chronological evaluation from validation.md. Historical LLM reconstruction stays contamination-sensitive. External validity requires real source retrieval and later outcomes; closed-world cases with supplied causes cannot establish it.

## Freeze the experiment

Before results, record question, population, selection rule, raw cases, procedure snapshots, prompts, tools/model settings actually exposed, resource budget, expected outcomes or rubric, tolerances, comparator, edits allowed, stopping rule and access limitations. Hash files for identity; do not claim hashes or separate folders enforce secrecy.

The baseline is the accepted pre-edit procedure, not a deliberately weakened caricature. Compare baseline and candidate on identical tasks, scope and tool budgets in fresh initial contexts. Preserve all responses; no best-of-N choice. If different cases are used, report separate robustness checks and do not call their score difference improvement. Make equal-quality findings explicit when baseline and candidate both pass.

Freeze development and reserve cases together. Reserve variants should change a meaningful constraint, timing or adverse alternative, not merely rename a company. Shared mechanisms mean near transfer, not unseen-domain performance. Once reserve results inform a change, that reserve becomes development data. Keep the failed result and require a new experiment for later evaluation.

Use a bounded correction budget set by task scope. A useful default is one general change batch followed by one reserve evaluation. Stop on completion or failure; do not keep rewriting until all known answers pass. Report developer/scorer access, author-generated cases and actual prompt versus enforced independence.

## Score the actions as well as the numbers

Predeclare applicable per-case checks, exact arithmetic/units/chronology tolerances, important causal decisions and critical errors. Retain traces of task selection, prerequisites, accepted versus rejected evidence, chosen assumption, forecast version, invalidated nodes, gate disposition and final claim. A statement promising to do these things is not an executed test.

Critical failures include invented observations/funding, after-cutoff ancestors, impossible balances/adoption, hidden causal residuals, false isolation/validation and substitution of intrinsic value for a horizon forecast. Excessive refusal on an anchored forecast is also a failed decision. Report per-family outcomes and all critical failures; an aggregate pass rate cannot offset one. Thresholds for synthetic engineering acceptance are not forecast confidence levels.

Separate: code safeguards; behavior under supplied facts; real retrieval/coverage; operating predictive accuracy; and price/ranking performance. A success in one cannot be promoted to another. Compare against run-rate, seasonal and guidance baselines only when those are meaningful for the actual forecast lane and dates.

## Refine general rules, not answers

Before editing, record observed failure, reproduction, mechanism causing it, candidate general correction, expected affected decisions and a negative-control case. Prefer a small executable invariant or clear task contract over another long checklist. Existing rules that were not executed need orchestration/acceptance repair; repeating their prose is not evidence of a fix.

An assumption must have a causal owner and scope; a result must satisfy its requested output type. Provisional authorization relaxes only the empirical-validation claim, not identity, arithmetic, chronology or physical feasibility. Current poor performance is neither a rejection rule nor a required recovery. A strong growth story is neither a rank nor proof that price will recognize it by the horizon.

Preserve baseline files, original forecasts, source versions and failed tests. Record changes in retrieval, causal model, recognition, valuation and mandate separately. The final report states what improved, what stayed equal, what failed, what was not tested and what would constitute the next external test. No trades, external publication or background evaluation are authorized by this process.
