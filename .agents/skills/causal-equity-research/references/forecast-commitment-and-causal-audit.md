# Committed forecasts, causal financial audit and market inflections

Operational v2.4, incorporating the substantive eight-case audit; originally v2.2, authorized by the user's 2026-11-03 correction. Applies to new work and explicit updates; do not rewrite frozen past forecasts. Read sections relevant to the task. This is a forecasting contract, not a direction to produce bullish answers.

## 1. Make a decision about the future

O00 must select one operating path and, for a requested provisional investment decision, one horizon market-price forecast when a defensible model can be built. State “I forecast X by date T because A leads to B.” Do not replace the forecast with a list of possibilities, a conditional intrinsic value, or repeated requests for more certainty. Keep deterministic sensitivities separate; no probability averaging, arbitrary execution haircut, or automatic pull toward today's earnings.

“On track” means the latest evidenced trajectory with known transitions and constraints: signed price resets, customer budgets, qualification milestones, demonstrated yields, seasonal patterns, funded capacity and realistic response lags. It does not mean flat forever, perfect management execution, every order converting, or all unknowns resolving positively. Apply the same continuity standard to favorable and adverse drivers. Announced capacity is not already qualified output. Do not introduce an unannounced accident or recession into the central path solely to be conservative.

For each material future unknown, first seek the cheapest discriminating evidence, then choose a point estimate from: a disclosed contractual schedule; demonstrated cohort/milestone trend; an appropriate causal analogue adjusted for known differences; or a transparent constrained inference. A management plan can anchor the path, labeled COMPANY_CLAIM and tested against resources and execution to date. Record why the selected estimate best fits the clues rather than selecting the pessimistic endpoint. No arbitrary midpoint or target-fitting. Endogenous growth, adoption, learning and recovery inferred from existing clues need not already be announced: they may enter as MODEL_ASSUMPTION with event knowledge class STRUCTURAL_INFERENCE. Keep evidence classification separate from event knowledge class. The stress-only restriction concerns unsupported discrete surprises, not ordinary forecasting.

Before choosing deterioration or recovery, reconcile the path to the latest comparable actuals and guidance. Align GAAP/adjusted definitions, fiscal periods, acquisition perimeter and product mix. Calculate the remaining-period rates implied by an annual forecast. Explain every material departure through quantities, prices, costs or timing; an older guidance midpoint is not a default after new evidence. Lack of proof of technical superiority does not determine margin erosion: qualification, scarce capacity, bargaining power, substitutes and rival response determine its magnitude and date. Apply the same burden of explanation to positive and negative transitions.

Use an assumption ledger:

`parameter_id; definition/units; as_of; observed_anchor/source_ids; classification; mechanism; chosen_value_by_period; continuation_or_transition_rule; necessary_resources; strongest_alternative; selection_reason; break_even; falsifier/next_release; downstream_nodes; owner`

Distinguish three cases:

| Case | Required action |
|---|---|
| Future outcome is unknown but anchored: yield, contract renewal, collection timing, qualified ramp | Choose a reasoned point path, expose assumption, calculate results and falsifier. Sensitivity alone does not block the provisional decision. |
| Historical amount, identity, units, ownership or accounting is missing/conflicted | Resolve the source; never call a fabricated replacement observed. Any explicitly estimated opening balance must be labeled, bounded and keep the dependent historical tie-out unpassed. |
| No defensible anchor, impossible physical balance, missing financing or mutually incompatible decisive facts | Identify the exact broken link. Retain usable upstream forecasts and route; do not invent the missing link to finish a target. |

The user's standing permission allows PROVISIONAL_UNVALIDATED judgments without established predictive accuracy. It does not pass G18/G20 or make a strict eligible ranking. For provisional work, an anchored future assumption is sufficient to complete a model even if a reasonable stress changes the preferred company. Explain that dependence once. Unsupported facts and impossible models remain disqualifying for the affected claim.

A13/A14 separately commit to the recognition path: what investors can learn by T, the fixed future earnings/cash denominator they are forecast to capitalize, and the evidence-backed valuation mapping. Public information is neither automatically priced nor automatically ignored. A DCF remains a cross-check; an economic value without a dated recognition mechanism is not a horizon market-price prediction. If a target cannot be supported, identify the missing recognition link rather than relabeling intrinsic value.

## 2. Explain levels, changes, and apparently unchanged numbers

A17 inventories every numeric item in the declared scope, including notes, segment metrics and meaningful omissions. A12 assigns each a driver or explicit residual, a causal status, and a forward disposition. Group immaterial items only with listed membership and reason; do not claim every datum is explained from a few headline ratios. Broad screening stays shallow; full financial causal census belongs to finalists or a requested issuer audit.

For each material line/metric record:

`metric_id; period/basis; prior/current; absolute_and_relative_change; source_ids; level_drivers; change_bridge; causal_status; counterevidence; unexplained_residual; next_quarter/half_year/year_value; driver_transition_dates; accounting/cash_lags; reversal_or_acceleration_trigger; affected_nodes`

Causal status distinguishes disclosed attribution, corroborated inference, and model hypothesis. Company commentary is evidence of management's explanation, not independent proof of causation. Reconcile to customers, rivals or physical data where possible. A residual remains visible and gets an owner; never use a plug to claim complete explanation. Do not mechanically extrapolate an unexplained historical residual.

For revenue, use price/volume/mix/product/cohort/FX and perimeter changes without double counting. For one product the exact bridge is `P0*dQ + Q0*dP + dP*dQ`; for many products use a stated exact sequential or index bridge with interactions and aggregation checked. For ratios, bridge numerator and denominator; a higher margin can result from shrinking low-margin sales. Match calendar/fiscal days, restatements and gross/net presentation. Assess materiality from gross offsetting exposures and decision sensitivity, not only the small net change; tiny amounts can signal a major transition. An immaterial classification is itself reasoned and inspectable.

Map costs to input vintage, hedges/contracts, yield/scrap, utilization/fixed absorption, freight, labor/productivity, warranty, depreciation, customer mix and by-product credits. Map operating expenses to headcount/pay, launches, restructuring, capitalization and stock compensation. Bridge tax, interest, noncontrolling claims, dilution and FX. For balance sheets and cash, separate physical inventory, valuation/write-down, acceptance, receivable aging, advances, payables, financing and settlement. A recognition shift is not new demand; a one-time cash release is not perpetual free cash flow.

For EVERY material changed or flat metric answer:
1. What sets its current level and what changed the underlying drivers?
2. What offsetting movements might conceal a change? Inspect segments/cohorts, units versus price, currency and accounting perimeter.
3. Which drivers persist, expire, accelerate, reverse or reach a threshold? At what date and through what mechanism?
4. What would change next quarter, half-year and year even if the latest reported total is flat?
5. Which measurement and cash dates differ, and what leading observation would invalidate the path?

An explanation of the past is incomplete until it produces a forward rule. “Revenue grew,” “cost rose,” “management is cautious,” and “margin may not persist” do not answer these questions.

Historical normalization and causal forecasting are separate deliverables. For a material weak, flat or improving metric, reconcile sequential and year-on-year changes where available, then carry the identified drivers into the next quarter, half-year and year. Separate accounting adjustments from operating change; a zero arithmetic residual does not establish economic attribution. Use segment/cohort offsets to expose hidden progress and deterioration. Classify costs as fixed, volume-linked, ramp/launch, recurring renewal or genuinely expiring before projecting operating leverage. For inventory and receivables, model conversion together with replenishment and continued growth funding; neither full cash release nor permanent cash burn follows from a balance alone.

## 3. Search deliberately for improvement before it is reported

Comparable screens must examine both (a) present growth that can accelerate/decelerate and (b) poor or ordinary performance whose causes can reverse. Do not require candidates from both categories if evidence supplies none; record the search and outcome. Neither current growth nor a falling share price proves opportunity.

Search for expiring launch/qualification costs, idle capacity awaiting funded demand, inventory destocking approaching completion, delayed acceptance/sales becoming recognized, input prices reaching cost of sales with a lag, contract repricing, yield learning, completed restructuring, adverse mix reversal and replacement cycles. Each improvement needs a specific cause, amount, date, required resources and evidence that the cause will change. Financially distressed demand, permanently lost customers and structural technical inferiority are not automatic mean-reversion cases.

Treat latent improvement as a discovery responsibility, not a caveat added after selecting current growers. Ask what restrains today's economics, what makes that restraint change, and how much incremental contribution could emerge before headline growth is visible. Ordinary margins can conceal a small profitable cohort becoming material, utilization nearing fixed-cost absorption, or a contract/cost vintage about to roll. An observed learning or maturation trajectory can support an estimated transition without a formal management announcement. Investigate these paths with the same effort and causal standard as possible decay in an existing winner; do not require recovery to appear in reported earnings before admitting the route.

A12 supplies an improvement/erosion register to A03/A14/A19; A23 propagates its mechanisms to related companies through the existing evidence map. A normal headline can hide a new high-margin cohort, near-full bottleneck or cost step-down. Compare incremental earnings and cash from that change against expectations and price before doing another round of accounting refinement.

## 4. Model the marginal balance and the clock

A05 builds consumption/procurement; A06 builds good deliverable output independently; A07 models price formation; A19 reconciles their state paths. Use product grade, geography, qualification and delivery date. Annual global totals cannot settle a local near-term shortage.

For each local market:

`I_end = I_open_usable + primary_good_supply + recycled_supply + imports - exports - consumption`

Keep transfers and global production separate; regional sums cancel trade, not create demand. Unusable, bonded, committed and exchange-warrant stocks need distinct availability assumptions. If opening inventory is already free/uncommitted, do not subtract commitments again. Preserve mine ore -> recovered metal -> concentrate -> refined metal -> fabricated product conversion and throughput lags. For semiconductors use the corresponding wafer/yield/package/qualified-system map.

Estimate buffer runway from usable stock above the operating floor divided by net draw for a constant-flow interval; integrate piecewise time-varying flows otherwise. Do not divide by gross demand or total inventories. Identify the first constraint date relative to replenishment, substitution and new capacity. A small global imbalance can have a large local price effect when the immediately deliverable buffer runs out. A concentrate bottleneck can coexist with refined-metal surplus; inventories can rise globally while falling in the location that sets the relevant price. Record each serial stage's equivalent good-output capacity, current binding stage, next binding stage and transition date. Expanding a nonbinding stage may add zero shipments; relieving the bottleneck can increase volume while lowering total profit through contract-price normalization. Recompute both quantity and price at each transition.

Before accepting the path check: time spreads and same-grade regional premia; exchange/free inventories and withdrawals; shipping/arbitrage after freight/tax/financing; treatment/refining charges and scrap response; customer lead times/orders/cancellations; funded downstream activity; producer recovery, grades and utilization. These are mechanism checks, not a universal indicator-score model. A price rally alone does not prove shortage; a low treatment charge alone does not prove a refined-copper shortage.

Construct the price path from evidenced contract resets, delivered alternatives, inventory regime and supply/demand responses. If elasticity is unknown, choose a supported relationship or analogue and show its break-even; never convert a tonnage deficit directly into an arbitrary percentage price increase. Demand destruction, substitution and recycling enter when their economics/lead times make them relevant. Separate a forecastable vulnerability from the exact trigger of an unannounced shock.

The market deliverable is a populated calculation, not a relationship diagram: dated consumption/procurement, equivalent qualified output, opening/closing usable stocks, contract-weighted prices and the binding constraint. Calculate a stock/qualification/price-reset transition date, or show why none occurs within the modeled interval. For services or treatment cohorts use the relevant funded-customer, throughput and retention state rather than inventing physical inventory. Carry the same accepted quantities and prices into company revenue and margin; identify residual mix/cost effects. If exact private data are unavailable, use anchored estimates at a decision-useful aggregation and preserve their status. More data requests do not replace a defensible estimate; an unsupported gap does not become a fact.

Translate through the value chain: upstream owners may gain, constrained producers may lose volume, refiners may lose treatment margin, and fabricators may face input cost/working-capital pressure or pass-through. A commodity rally is not a buy signal for every producer. Knowledge-map coverage can extend beyond the selectable exchange universe.

Accept useful accounting or discovery work for that narrower scope while finishing the forecast. Downside sensitivities do not vote on the central path; choose that path from evidence and report its risks separately.

Use existing records with these additional fields rather than creating an independent competing model. All scoped observations retain provenance and classifications. Keep evidence gates, numeric integrity and empirical validation status distinct from conviction in the chosen forecast.

Behavior tests must include: an anchored future unknown that requires a point forecast; flat totals with opposing drivers; a negative metric with an expiring cause; deliverable local stock exhaustion despite global surplus; and a genuine structural deterioration that must not be forced into recovery. Freeze raw cases/rubrics before testing, keep synthetic data outside live evidence, and preserve failures. A retrospective copper explanation is not a successful ex-ante prediction without an original timestamped forecast.
