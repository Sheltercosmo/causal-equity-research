# Quantitative market research and execution history

O00 commissions this work when a market mechanism could change admission, deferral or the forecast. Scale to the decision. At triage calculate the binding threshold. Before a finalist receives a target, populate the model by application, grade, geography, customer and period at the granularity that changes its economics. Preserve reusable source IDs, units, currencies, vintages, comparable alternatives, the calculation and its dependents. A table of indicators alone cannot establish a market balance.

## Reconstruct the market twice

1. Before looking at the issuer target or peer conclusions, A05 reconstructs what customers are willing and able to deploy. A06 independently reconstructs what rival and captive producers can actually deliver. A08 reads the relevant full papers, test methods, product specifications, errata and customer qualification evidence where technology determines the result. A09/A18 connect that evidence to the buyer's budget and alternative. Obtain A11's plans and execution record separately. First passes use source manifests and clean contexts.
2. For each decisive input A01/A17/A23 search the relevant issuer footnote and historical release, the buyer's procurement/payment or commissioning record, competing suppliers and an independent physical series or matched engineering measurement. Extend to permitting, utilities, logistics, lenders, government registers, trade flows, component makers or operators where they can resolve the bottleneck. Search in the relevant language and at the product and facility level. Record what was read, its locator, first-public date, data interval, origin, units and coverage. Trace a copied figure to its origin. A customer and supplier describing one contract confirm execution from different sides but represent one order. Date qualitative claims, leaks and contrary evidence separately. A conversion inferred from an aggregate needs a second route with different inputs where feasible.
3. Reconcile the two estimates before seeing valuation. Report measured, derived and assumed cells, parameter definitions and source IDs. For inconsistent numbers decompose perimeter, unit, generation mix, captive use, revenue-recognition basis and lag. Quantify the unexplained residual and the decision threshold it could cross. Preserve disagreement where an independent measurement is unavailable. Prioritize the obtainable observation most likely to change the sign of the conclusion.

## Equations must describe the business

Demand and supply can differ. Cross-validate each independently and reconcile their difference through price, inventories, queues and substitution. For example, 110 paid requests, 90 qualified new units and five starting units above reserve permit 95 deliveries and 15 queued before cancellations. Forcing the two input estimates to agree would erase the physical finding.

Build a time-indexed model in the native unit, and show enough substitution of sourced numbers for a second analyst to reproduce it. A quarter is often sufficient for demand. Use weekly or monthly steps through a supply shock, commissioning, inventory exhaustion or contract reset, then aggregate flows across nonoverlapping periods. Publish next quarter, rolling six months and rolling twelve months from the same state path. Retain opening/ending stocks, not sums of period-end stocks. Replace each equation below with the actual production process, contract or customer choice where appropriate.

| Decision | Minimum useful calculation |
|---|---|
| Value to the buyer | For matched quality, reliability and workload, calculate discounted incremental gross contribution plus avoidable operating costs, less acquisition, installation, migration, parallel operation, downtime, retraining, financing, replacements and exit friction relative to the best feasible alternative. Count recovered capacity only if it can sell output or avoid real expenditure. Solve break-even purchase price, utilization or useful life. Separate switching **in** from switching **out**, customer surplus from supplier capture, and technical performance from accepted deployment. |
| Paid demand | For each customer cohort, derive financed additions from funded projects, adoption among customers clearing the economics, complementary labor/power/installation slots and replacement of expiring cohorts. Use actual intersections or label a `min()` of counts as an upper bound. Product requirement equals deployable systems × qualified content per system plus independently identified replacement/spares. Translate use to procurement via desired inventory change and order-to-acceptance lag. Do not multiply percentages from overlapping cohorts or count buyers' total capex as product sales. |
| Effective supply | For each project and stage, map input throughput × uptime × good yield × product allocation into equivalent finished units using the bill of materials. Include opening work-in-process, transit, stage lags and acceptance where timing matters. Allocate shared tool-hours, wafer starts or technicians only once. Constrain finished output by the binding stage. Maintenance, learning, rival switching, capacity qualification and retirements each have a dated effect. Design/nameplate capacity and cumulative installed assets have different units from saleable units per period. |
| Market clearing | At a candidate delivered price, paid requests include new demand plus carried orders, less cancellations/substitution. Available supply includes good deliveries plus usable starting stock above reserve. Fulfilled units are limited by both and must obey grade/customer allocation and contract priority. Carry ending stock and unfilled orders separately. Apply imports, transfers, exports and scrap exactly once at the relevant boundary. Derive price from documented contract resets, auctions, competing delivered offers, value of an avoided delay or justified local demand/supply curves. With sticky pricing a shortage can produce a queue rather than higher ASP. Show which buffer is exhausted first, when, and what replaces it. |
| Share and moat | Allocate funded cohorts across qualified alternatives at their net benefit, switching calendar, multi-sourcing commitments and capacity. Unit share equals accepted supplier units divided by matched market units. Model erosion, improvement or retention by cohort. Independently compare the forecast with design wins progressing to accepted units, win/loss records, renewal cohorts, observed substitutions or matched-workload benchmarks. Explain a variance and reopen the affected assumption. |
| Issuer realization | Revenue is the sum of units accepted × realized contract price by grade plus separately identified services/fees. Show variable costs, idle capacity, fixed-cost absorption, reinvestment, tax/credits, compensation, working capital, financing and dilution. Recognized revenue, accounting profit and collected cash have their own clocks. Compare results with independent statement trends and management-implied remaining-period requirements. Reinvest future profits where growth needs capacity. |

For services use jobs/hours, sessions, seats or claims times the accepted price. Capacity expires when unused. Carry waiting customers and churn instead of manufacturing inventory. For a commodity with responsive prices, recompute substitution, marginal supply and imports at the new delivered price. For custom silicon, infer chips by generation from the revenue actually booked on packages or racks, then model memory, packaging, usable power, commissioning and sustained customer throughput separately. Peak compute equivalents are a technical measure. Incremental paid usage requires an additional bridge.

Always vary one demand input and one supply input independently in their natural units. Show the first affected flow, buffer or wait time, the supplier response, reset date, unit margin, cash, and the first disclosure that could move beliefs. Solve the smallest change that reverses the investment conclusion. Test one nonbinding input and one reversal or substitution. With a sparse private coefficient, anchor a central estimate to existing clues and show the competing result at an evidence-based bound.

## Keep executives' words and delivery on the same calendar

A01 records one row per distinct claim with claim ID, speaker/role, exact release date, metric/definition/unit, geography/product, target period or due date, guidance low/high/point or stated milestone, conditions, and the original source. Include relevant buyer, rival, regulator and technical outlooks with origin IDs. Record the contemporaneous comparison baseline and distinguish issuer guidance from the analyst’s independent forecast. Record revisions as new dated rows linked to the original. Scope the whole comparable series before scoring. Include cuts, withdrawals, delayed launches, misses, beats, qualitative claims and pending commitments.

A11 joins each matured claim to the first comparable actual and public date, later restatements separately, and the associated real-world delivery or cash where available. For numerical guidance report signed error `actual − original point`, relative error only on a meaningful nonzero base, distance outside a stated range, and change versus last revision. For deadlines report actual date less promised date in days, or an overdue lower bound through cutoff. Pending rows have no realized error. Disclose numerator, resolved count, unresolved count and horizon. Do not count repeated guidance for the same target as independent successes. A price reaction has its own event clock, comparison cohort and confounders.

A11 distinguishes missed resources, qualification, commissioning, demand shifts, changed mix, perimeter and accounting from discretionary execution. Show exactly which future ramp, cost, milestone or conversion rate the record changes, and when an unrelated comparison is excluded. A perfect streak on a small sample cannot generate a general trust score or an automatic earnings uplift. A negative or flat report can conceal a funded expansion or an easing constraint. Record the measured leading change and the next observation that can disprove it. On a later requested update, score the locked row first, then create a new forecast version with the new evidence.

## Cross the business forecast with expectations

O00 sets `run.business_contract = "2.8"` when locking a new publication packet. Future method changes compare the working procedure with the standing principles in the project instructions. For each affected principle give the preserved decision, an adverse or nonbinding control, and the observed result. Changing an entry in a checklist alone cannot demonstrate preservation. Keep the user's principles at the top of the skill and mark any user-directed amendment with its date and decision.

A16 also reproduces the *construction* of each load-bearing exit multiple, duration, contract payoff or discount parameter from its source inputs. Track all cutoff subject-quote fact IDs in `subject_quote_fact_ids` and the independent target parameters in `valuation_parameter_fact_ids`. Traverse their ancestors. A frozen factor built as today's quote divided by the analyst's own EPS still encodes that quote. Supply independently sourced forward capitalization terms. The price-implied alternative belongs in the expectations comparison. Review the model code and original sources for hidden constants or relabeled quote facts. For a KPI at a physical boundary, use one feasible adverse or favorable change and the unchanged boundary state with `boundary_reason` and `boundary_fact_ids`. For a fixed offer use a documented contract condition or timing variable if more meaningful than an operating KPI, specify success and failure values where relevant, and separately reconcile the actual closing terms.

A12 locks the customer/market/financial forecast without the favored valuation. A13/A14 independently explain what the current price could imply, the competing explanation for underperformance, and which fact before the horizon will change which fixed-period belief. Show customer/competitor disagreement, possible overreaction, and why a continuing discount may be justified. A rolling-year change or multiple change has an explicit denominator and economic cause.

Obtain the target from the earnings, cash or assets investors are expected to recognize at the horizon and a supported valuation relationship. For example, `T = fixed-year recognized EPS × justified exit P/E`, or `T = (capitalized operating value + available cash − debt − senior claims) / diluted shares`. Appropriate sector methods and enforceable consideration are allowed. Compare the operating result against cash-flow value, peer/issuer multiples corrected for accounting and durability, and the reverse expectations at today's quote. Reconcile disagreement by returning to the source driver. The subject quote and an assumed return on holding it may be reported as simple baselines only.

A16 performs same-model interventions. Hold operations, priced beliefs, capitalization terms and the information path fixed and change only the comparison quote. The numerator must reproduce the same horizon price. Then hold the original quote/beliefs fixed and perturb a decision-sensitive operating driver up and down. Trace the first meaningful change even where reduced price offsets volume, contracts protect earnings, or the horizon reveals little. Evidence must support a zero recognition effect. A genuine deal payoff has an independent contract plus timing/conditions and a failure path where material. When the subject quote itself affects real financing or dilution, freeze those states for the pure quote test and separately model that economic channel. O00 rejects an arbitrary quote-plus-carry drift even if the implied multiple looks reasonable.
