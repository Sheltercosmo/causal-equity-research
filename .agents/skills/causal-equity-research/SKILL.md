## The user's standing investment principles

Start with the buyer. What outcome does the product deliver against the best feasible alternative, and what does the customer pay to buy, implement, switch, operate and eventually leave? Use matched technical evidence and realized customer choices to forecast competitiveness, moat, its erosion and the supplier's share of the value. Quantify funded demand and qualified supply through time. Determine the resulting price, market share and contribution after competitive and management responses. Test management's incentives, actual execution and use of capital against its dated commitments. Reconcile those economics to future earnings and cash. Independently identify the market's prevailing expectation, why a gap exists, what can change it and when. Cross-check the operating and financial paths before setting a horizon price.

These principles come from the user. Every future method revision must show their continued effect on research decisions and test a counterexample that could reveal drift. O00 may improve tools and procedures. A substantive change to the principles requires explicit user direction. The subject's quote sets the comparison price and constrains plausible investor beliefs. Adding carry to that quote is permitted only as a benchmark. The same applies when the analyst expects few disclosures. Derive any flat target independently. An enforceable deal or redemption term can determine a security's payoff under its stated conditions.

## Objective

Unless the user specifies otherwise:

`price_upside = target_price_at_T / reference_price_at_t0 - 1`

T is the last session of the relevant exchange on or before the date two calendar months after t0. Verify the published exchange calendar. Freeze common information cutoff, reference-price time, timezone, currency, security/share class, corporate-action basis, universe and tradability constraints. The information set must be available by the reference time for an executable/as-of claim; later information requires a fresh reference or exclusion. Do not label a stale reference current.

Rank eligible candidates only by this price-appreciation measure. Use one explicit operating and expectation path and one point target per candidate. Disclose dividends, downside, dilution, liquidity, business quality, and deterministic perturbations separately; do not probability-weight the ranking or introduce an inherited benchmark/Omega/maximum-entry-price objective.

Model the business beyond the price window. Separate economic reality, corporate execution, accounting measurement, and investor beliefs. Each target needs an identifiable in-window information event that can change beliefs about future economics. A later capacity ramp can matter if news about it arrives before T.

## Run sequence

1. **Lock the run.** Record `work_mode` (broad search, issuer diligence, update, attribution, or demonstration) and `data_mode` (`LIVE` or `SYNTHETIC`), mandate, source versions, forecast lane, disclosure scope and available access isolation. A20 preregisters validation before any test access; A22 checks timestamp and label boundaries. Create an empty run under `research/runs/<run-id>/`; never seed it with former assistant recommendations.
2. **Discover, reuse and map.** A23 checks accepted evidence by scope and vintage before new retrieval. A02/A03/A04 split broad markets into applications/grades/cohorts, follow upstream/downstream/substitute links and explore unrelated pools. Map physical, payment and information flows. Full broad-search defaults remain 12 pools/about 60 names/roughly six finalists, transparently adjusted to scope. Counts do not prove completeness.
3. **Compare before deepening.** Produce comparable opportunity cards and forward state sheets before full issuer reconstruction. Include customer surplus/capture, next-quarter/half-year/year demand and supply, rival response, funding, strongest counter-case, conditional valuation requirements and an initial recognition route. A23 prioritizes discriminating questions before targets exist; numerical target sensitivity is used later. Compare continuing-growth routes with evidenced improvement hidden in negative, flat or ordinary results; investigate the cause and transition before requiring a reported recovery. Advance through bounded mechanism sprints. Preserve promising deferred routes with explicit triggers and review the revisit queue at each frontier comparison.
4. **Build causal economics.** A08 independently reads full relevant technical papers/specifications under matched workloads. A09/A18 quantify customer value, purchase and switching costs, feasible price bounds, affordability and deployable cohorts. A05/A06 independently build demand and qualified supply; A07/A11/A19 reconcile inventory, contracts, pricing, rational responses and structural/transient changes. A10 derives share. Required investment and future supplier value capture follow these mechanisms; current cash yield is not a discovery veto.
5. **Deepen and challenge finalists.** A01/A17/A22 expand the scoped corpus and census as a route advances; every item in the declared scope still receives a disposition. Finalists prefer five annual periods/28 usable quarters through a prior cycle, with two relevant nodes per chain direction or labeled proxies and all material concentrated-market rivals. A12 gives every scoped numeric disclosure a causal/forward disposition, reconciles material changed and flat metrics to drivers, and forecasts statements from accepted drivers; A15/A16 test both causal loops independently. Before each forecast lock, A19/A23 record the forward-factor sweep, inclusion/exclusion decisions and lags. O00 adjudicates competing operating paths through the v2.4 reconciliation in orchestration.md before A13 finalizes valuation; accounting adjustments alone do not complete the financial task. Reopen the owning mechanism when accounts disagree; do not tune a target or arbitrary multiple.
6. **Validate honestly.** Read [validation.md](references/validation.md). A20 freezes protocol; A22 produces cutoff-legal feature packets; forecasts and their dependencies are hashed before outcomes; A21 scores once when access and timing support the claim. Keep structural forecasts, guidance-assisted nowcasts, post-release attribution and prospective shadow distinct. Historical LLM work is contamination-sensitive. Synthetic tests establish only the arithmetic and workflow behavior exercised; they do not establish market predictive accuracy.
7. **Construct the target.** A14 maps new information to changed future beliefs. A13 constructs a fixed-year earnings/multiple bridge or appropriate sector valuation, cross-checking market/share economics and properly discounted cash. No analyst price targets or direct analyst revenue/EPS forecasts enter the investment model. Multiples need issuer history, matched peers and economic sustainability. Do not compare trailing and forward denominators as if unchanged.
8. **Review, publish and preserve routes.** A15 attacks decisive premises; A16 audits arithmetic, chronology and comparability. O00 adjudicates G00–G24 globally and per finalist. Strict rankings require eligible candidates. When the user explicitly permits a provisional selection, record that mandate amendment and publish it separately as an unvalidated judgment with exact open gates; never mark validation PASS or manufacture missing business evidence. Before concluding that no attractive case exists, revisit deferred routes and distinguish unresearched, contradicted, mistimed and unattractively priced cases.

## Evidence and model invariants

- Classify inputs as `OBSERVED`, `COMPANY_CLAIM`, `DERIVED`, `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `CONTRADICTED`. Keep model outputs and expectation judgments distinct from observations.
- Preserve source IDs, exact locators, observation intervals, first-public and provider-available timestamps, retrieval time, origin clusters, original/revised versions and recursive lineage. Mirrors are one evidence origin. A derived value is unavailable if any required ancestor is after cutoff.
- Scoped census dispositions: `MAPPED`, `EXCLUDED_REASONED`, `CONFLICT_OPEN`, `UNREADABLE`, `UNREVIEWED`. Complete disposition does not resolve a critical unreadable item. Missing is never zero.
- Customer economics uses incremental contribution and avoidable cash costs against the best feasible alternative, including defer/repair/in-house. Avoid double-counted labor, energy and capacity benefits. Positive NPV alone does not imply adoption; calculate customer-set intersections and physical slots.
- Conserve stock and flows; sum flows, carry ending stocks, weight rates and ASPs correctly. Use minimum equivalent throughput for serial bottlenecks and shared machine-hours for competing grades. Negative inventory requires resolution. A shortage alone cannot determine price.
- Separate order, announcement, price-effective, procurement, delivery/acceptance, revenue recognition, cash and investor-recognition clocks. Known future factors enter the path with supported lags. Unannounced shocks remain deterministic stresses; post-origin realizations cannot repair frozen forecasts.
- Revenue/share is an output of cohort choices, procurement and qualified supply. Distinguish unit/revenue share, merchant/captive supply, private competitors and replacements. Reconcile peer allocations and financial conversion.
- Preserve competing explanations and choose one forward path. Missing or contradictory historical facts block dependent factual claims. Forecastable unknowns use explicit on-track estimates anchored to existing clues, with mechanism, scale, timing and falsifier; ranking sensitivity triggers research and a disclosed break-even, not automatic abstention or a downside haircut. Follow the v2.2 assumption contract; never conceal unsupported data or claim predictive certainty.
